# Interlude: The Wizard's Tower

---

The evening at the [[Cross Bones Pub]] had started like any other — until the door opened.

The figure that entered moved with unsettling deliberateness, scanning the room before crossing to the adventurers' table. What had seemed at first like some strange creature resolved into something stranger: a child. Or something shaped like one. [[Lilith]] was one hundred and twelve years old, held in a child's body by a condition she neither dwelt on nor explained. She wanted work, she said. She didn't much care what kind. Her interview answer — *raise money, and I don't care how* — was good enough.

[[Snag Ironvow]] spent part of his welcome on lucky dip bags from Jacinto the Stuff Merchant. Everyone did. [[Jinx]] got an onion, a vial of blood, and fourteen gold pieces. [[Lilith]] won a loyal umbrella that burst from the bag at full size and immediately began hovering above her head. [[Snag Ironvow]] unwrapped a quill that rewrote his sentences whenever it disagreed with him.

Then the thunder came.

Blue lightning split the sky above the outskirts of [[Thornhaven]], followed by a beam of light so steady it looked deliberate — rising from, or descending to, the old Wizard's Tower on the edge of town. A retired adventurer named Ham Door Stopper had visited the tower once, years ago, and found only dust and a door he could not open. What [[Nyra]]'s navigation brought them to two hours later bore no resemblance to his story: clean-cut stone, ornate copper wiring tracing the exterior, a copper tile roof. The front door was unlocked. The fire inside was lit.

They worked through the tower in the dark. Upstairs, a bedroom: crystals, robes, a journal belonging to one [[Perseus Perchance]] — entries about something called Matilda, and pages obsessing over a power close enough to touch. Downstairs, a cavern, and [[Matilda]] herself: an enormous Otyugh with pink bows tied around her tentacles and a persistent, urgent hunger. [[Jinx]]'s lucky dip onion turned out to be the right gift.

[[Nyra]]'s Detect Magic found the portal — a painting on the ground floor, depicting a bright workshop humming with something very old.

On the other side was the wizard.

[[Perseus Perchance]] had spent centuries condensing Ley line energy into a single power source, a ball of concentrated blue lightning locked in a copper cage at the centre of his workshop. He had barely noticed Thornhaven existed. When [[Jinx]] produced [[Toodle]] — dark and silent since [[X01 Mr Mayfair's Magical Mystorium|the night of the fair]] — Perseus extracted a sliver of that condensed energy and pressed it into her chest.

Toodle's eyes opened. She asked about Mr Mayfair.

She did not ask about the sound the copper cage made afterward. The sliver had cost more than anyone intended. The tower began to come apart — walls splitting, the painting falling, the beam of light above sputtering into ordinary dark. They climbed out through windows and the crumbling frame as centuries of patient work became rubble at their feet.

Perseus stood in the ruins with [[Matilda]] at his side. His notes were gone. His tower was gone. He declined the invitation to Thornhaven. There were Ley lines to find.

By dawn the party rode back into the city. Mayor Emma met them with fifty gold pieces each and considerable relief. The light was gone. The mystery was resolved — or as resolved as it was going to get.

[[Toodle]] floated above [[Jinx]]'s shoulder the whole ride home, warm and ticking with something older than any of them.
