# Chapter Seven: Return to Thornhaven

---

They reached [[Thornhaven]]'s west gate at first light, carrying their attuned items and the weight of Pipe's choice. The city hadn't changed — but the young Dragonborn guard at the gate greeted them by reputation, not suspicion, and delivered the Mayor's invitation with a smile too polished to be coincidental.

[[Mirr]] spotted the escort for what it was. She diverted one of the guards — a cheerful young man the party quickly nicknamed Daddy — into the morning market for a beer. He took it well. He also, over his second cup, mentioned an underground gathering in [[Aussen]] they might enjoy. His invitation was still in the air when the party crouched near a market stall to examine a ceramic talisman. The maker's mark was familiar: the stamp of [[The Sealed Workshop]], a building that had been chained shut by regional authority for thirty years. The clay in the grooves was still clean.

---

[[Eman]] received them at the [[TownHall]] in a room that still smelled of cold tea. She looked like someone who had not slept and had spent the sleepless hours making peace with it. She was warm, almost disarmingly so — she acknowledged that Cross Bones had overstepped, accepted that [[Pipe Ashford]] had made her choice, and asked only for discretion. She paid fifty gold pieces per head. [[Aeris Whisperwind]] left the meeting with a seat on the city's future council. [[Mirr]]'s hand drifted to her Pactbreaker Shard twice during the conversation. Both times it was cold.

On the eastern edge of the city, [[Roderick Ashford]] opened his door slowly. His eyes searched the street behind the party for someone who wasn't there. [[Selune]] placed the letter in his hands. He read it twice. His hands shook, but he was smiling by the end — *"She sounds like her mother."* He had been keeping the contract money set aside since the day he hired them. He paid without being asked, then disappeared into a back room and returned with a small blue tin. Inside: a candle his wife Maren had made, a kitchen charm that had kept their house honest for thirty years. A Zone of Truth — and a curse, he warned with a faint smile. Everyone in the room cries.

---

The [[Scarsith Temple]] smelled of tallow and bread. [[Nell]] served a modest meal and spoke at length about the lower districts before arriving at her ask: carry a census message to the eastern farms, discreetly, because [[Eman]]'s people had taken to watching her couriers. Throughout the meal, an old priest named [[Brother Toben]] sat in the corner and said nothing. He watched [[Selune]] with the patience of someone who had been holding a question for years. When the others drifted away, he crossed the room and stood before her.

*"The silver eclipse. Where did you see it?"*

He waited.

Later, at the [[Cross Bones Pub]], [[Mills]] was in her usual corner with drinks already poured. She acknowledged the conflict with her crew without bitterness and made her pitch: travel to the Thelasian ruins near [[Aussen]], find out what [[Dr Clair Bucklebub]] was pulling out of the earth, and recover what was already reaching the morning market. A hundred gold on return with an honest report. She gave them until dawn.

---

That night, Aeris played a song about a woman who had lost her mother too young and spent her life searching for an answer nature hadn't given her. She named no one. She revealed nothing about where the woman had gone. And when she looked up from the final chord, she saw a face across the crowd — still, watching — that she placed at Elarion's high court on the night her father Thalen Rook gave his last performance. The figure held her gaze for one moment, then was gone.

The owl came after dark. It landed on [[Selune]]'s windowsill, left a single white feather, and departed without a sound. By morning, there were four roads out of Thornhaven. The party chose the forest.
