## 👨‍🎤 Description

A 26-year-old halfling with soft grey-brown feathers growing through the skin of her left arm — elbow to shoulder. The feathers are rooted in her skin the way they belong on a bird, not attached on top. A partial wingspan is visible behind her back; she cannot fly. The transformation is ongoing.

Small, quick, and dressed in layers of clothing that accommodate her changing shape. Cheerful and dry. Unbothered by most things.

## 📝 Notes

- Scout and first contact for the Owl Cult settlement in [[Halfheight Forest]]
- Led the party to the settlement after observing them deal with the Root Beetle nest — she had been watching all night
- Had planned to send a cleanup crew to deal with the alarm-web nest; acknowledged the party solved it (violently, without warning) which is basically the same as helping
- Confirmed the Root Beetle problem is linked to the roots humming wrong for about a year; the crimson silk has been spreading toward the lake
- Name was passed along to [[Selune]] by a tree during Speak with Plants — the party did not know this and found the connection unsettling
