## 👨‍🎤 Description

Silver-white light shaped vaguely like an elf woman, barely material. Taller than [[Mirr]]. She has been in the underground chamber below the Silver Eclipse glade for thirty years, waiting.

She speaks throughout. She addresses people as if she already knows them.

## 📝 Notes

- Guardian of the underground chamber alongside Keth; held the ritual seal from the interrupted Circle of the Silver Eclipse ritual
- Her role: recognise genuine intent, release the seal when proved, lift the blood root curse layer from the party's attuned items
- In S09: touched [[Selune]]'s forehead and released the seal, completing the interrupted thirty-year-old encoding; simultaneously lifted the first of five blood root curse layers from the party's items
- Gave the party a bracelet of knotted grass on the way out — allows one reroll per day before seeing the pass/fail result; the new roll must be used
- Companion guardian: Keth (the stone construct who sets the physical terms of the test)
