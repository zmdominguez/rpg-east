## 👨‍🎤 Description

A stone construct — enormous, bark and granite fused together over centuries, humanoid in shape but barely. Something that was once a druid. Present in the underground chamber directly below the Silver Eclipse standing stone. Unrecognisable as a living thing until he begins to rise from the floor.

## 📝 Notes

- Guardian of the underground chamber below the Silver Eclipse glade; has held this post for thirty years
- Introduced himself by name and offered three options — fight, leave something of value, or leave
- Held the ritual seal from the interrupted Circle of the Silver Eclipse ritual until the vessel ([[Selune]]) returned to receive it
- Companion guardian: Silael (an elf-spirit in silver and white, who released the seal and lifted the first blood root curse layer from the party's attuned items)
- Accepted the party's offerings as proof of genuine intent: a song written on the spot, a bound feather, an offer of loyalty, and one very good stick
