## 👨‍🎤 Description

2.4 metres tall. Great grey barn owl, white heart-shaped face, forward-facing black eyes large enough to absorb the light around them. Classified as Huge by any reasonable measure. Makes the glade feel correctly scaled.

He was perched above the Silver Eclipse stone when the party arrived. His first words were directed only at [[Selune]]: *"You carry the stone's smell."*

Speaks in short statements with long pauses. States things; does not explain them. Watches reactions more than he responds to questions.

## 📝 Notes

- Permanently owl — has been an owl for longer than anyone in the settlement can remember
- Was present at the Silver Eclipse ritual thirty years ago; witnessed the crimson flash that scattered the druid circle
- Recognised [[Selune]] as the child who was taken before the ritual could be completed
- His understanding of the interruption is partial — the flash damaged his memory of the event. He remembers a figure moving and the colour of the light, not the name or the face
- Told [[Selune]] in S09: *"The ritual was interrupted. Someone came who should not have been there. The circle scattered. I thought the child was lost. You are not lost."*
- Gave the party a crystal eye (surveillance ball, visual feed only) as a gift at the end of S09
