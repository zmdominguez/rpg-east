## 👨‍🎤 Description
- Tall, handsome man; well-dressed and studious
- Works with copper instruments and alchemical equipment
- Highly educated and easily excited by his research — somewhat oblivious to anything outside it

## 📝 Notes
- Has spent centuries condensing Ley line energy in a tower outside [[Thornhaven]]
- Researches Ley lines — ancient sources of magical energy that crisscross the world; only certain individuals can tap them
- Lived in his tower with his pet [[Matilda]], an Otyugh; cared for her deeply
- His tower was destroyed in [[X02 The Wizard's Tower]] when a sliver of condensed Ley energy was extracted to repower [[Toodle]]
- Claims to be "well-published" but his tower (and all his unpublished research) is now rubble
- Refused to enter [[Thornhaven]]; departed immediately to find a new Ley line source
- His journal mentions being "so close" to stabilising Ley line energy into a true, usable power source
