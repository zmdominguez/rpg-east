## 👨‍🎤 Description

A stone construct, humanoid in shape. Present in the underground chamber beneath the Silver Eclipse stone in [[Halfheight Forest]]. Waited patiently while the party deliberated and introduced himself by name.

## 📝 Notes

- Guardian of the underground chamber below the Silver Eclipse glade; has held his post for thirty years
- First words to the party: offered three options — fight, leave something of value, or leave
- The guardians held the seal from the interrupted ritual until the vessel returned to claim it
- Companion guardian: Silly (an elf in silver and white, who lifted the first layer of the curse from [[Selune]])
- Accepted the party's offerings as proof of intent: a song written on the spot, a bound feather, an offer of loyalty, and one very good stick
