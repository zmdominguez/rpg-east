## 👨‍🎤 Description

An old, weathered priest — slow-moving, rarely seen outside the [[Scarsith Temple]]'s inner rooms. Quiet to the point of invisibility. When he does speak, it carries weight.

## 📝 Notes

- A long-standing member of the Scarsith congregation in [[Thornhaven]]
- Sat in silence throughout the party's meal with [[Nell]] in S07, watching [[Selune]] with unsettling patience
- After the meal, approached Selune alone with a single question: *"The silver eclipse. Where did you see it?"*
- Has been waiting a very long time for exactly this moment — he recognises something in Selune connected to records older than Thornhaven's founding
