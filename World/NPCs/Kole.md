## 👨‍🎤 Description

Broad-shouldered, human in shape, with yellow eyes and the quality of attention most people only reach when afraid. He is not afraid. He stands with the patience of something that has been watching a doorway for a very long time.

One of the original Owl Cult members. He has not fully transformed — or has transformed in ways that do not show on the outside. His posture, his stillness, and his eyes say otherwise.

## 📝 Notes

- Threshold guardian of the Owl Cult settlement; stands at the path to the inner glade
- His test for outsiders: *"Outsiders come here to take. What would you leave?"*
  - Not a riddle. He wants to know whether you understand what you are approaching
  - The settlement has been robbed by city people before — they ate the food, left with a recovered bowl and a carved spear
- Accepted the party's offerings in S09 and stepped aside; followed at a distance for the rest of the evening without saying another word
- Pip warned the party about him: *"Kole. Don't worry, the Elder said to bring them."* — and then made her peace with whatever was about to happen
