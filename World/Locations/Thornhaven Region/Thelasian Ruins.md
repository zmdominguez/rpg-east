## Description

Underground settlement of the ancient Thelasian civilization, accessed via a stone tablet in the [[Dirsty Swamp]]. A spiral staircase leads down into bioluminescent caverns.

## Key Features

- **Spiral staircase:** The main descent from the surface entrance
- **Bioluminescent caverns:** Lit by glowworms; eerie and disorienting
- **Cleansing pools:** Ritual or functional pools found within the settlement
- **Dangerous fungi:** Spores that cause temporary blindness

## Key NPCs

- [[Dr Clair Bucklebub]] — Halfling archaeologist leading ongoing excavations
