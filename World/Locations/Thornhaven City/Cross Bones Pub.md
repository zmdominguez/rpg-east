History
> Was owned by [[Mills Senior]], he was chosen as the first tavern keeper of the city. The first decoration tried to bring the pub a fancy vibe. Under [[Mills Senior]], the establishment was known as the **Cross Inn** — named after the crossing street at its corner.

> When [[Mills]] (Jr.) inherited the business, he renamed it the **Cross Bones Pub**. The new name keeps the "cross" reference to the street but layers in the bones of adventures — trophies, relics, and the stories that adventurers bring through the door — arranged in a crossed pattern like a pirate's mark.

> Alongside the pub, [[Mills]] also runs **Cross Bones Inc**, a separate incorporation that handles adventuring contracts and party logistics. The two businesses share a name and a roof but operate independently.
