Status: Destroyed
Region: Outskirts of [[Thornhaven]]
Resident: [[Perseus Perchance]] (departed)

## Notes
- A tall stone tower on the outskirts of [[Thornhaven]], predating the city's founding
- Known by locals to be an abandoned ruin, but secretly restored and inhabited by [[Perseus Perchance]] using Ley line energy
- Exterior featured ornate copper wiring tracing up the walls and a copper tile roof; no windows
- Topped by a beam of blue light (the Ley line spotlight) visible from the city
- Interior included a cosy living room, a bedroom laboratory, and a basement cavern housing [[Matilda]]
- A magical painting served as a portal to Perseus's private workshop

## History
- Destroyed in [[X02 The Wizard's Tower]] when extracting a sliver of condensed Ley line energy to repower [[Toodle]] destabilised Perseus's centuries of accumulated power
- The tower reverted to its original ruined state before collapsing entirely
