## 🕯️ Backstory

Lilith is 112 years old, though she has never looked it. A condition she declines to explain has kept her in the body of a child for her entire life. Her parents are "long gone." She does not elaborate, and she asks that no one point out the contradiction between her appearance and her claimed age.

She came to [[Thornhaven]] looking for work. She found [[Cross Bones Inc]] instead.

## 👨‍🎤 Description

Appears entirely as a small child. The only things that do not quite fit are her eyes, which have seen more decades than most adults, and her tendency to use vocabulary that a child her apparent age should not know.

Carries a **Loyal Umbrella** that hovers above her whenever opened and cannot be closed for 24 hours.
