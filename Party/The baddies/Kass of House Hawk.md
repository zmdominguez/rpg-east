## 🕯️ Backstory
### The Aerie of Skyreach
High above the tree line, where the wind never stops moving, sits [[Skyreach]] . a village carved into the cliffs and crowns of an ancient mountain range. Its people are all Aarakocra, but not of one bloodline. Generations ago, a scattering of celestial-touched families settled the peaks together, each bearing the mark of a different bird of prey: **House Hawk, House Falcon, House Owl, House Raven, House Eagle.** Each House carries the temperament, instincts, and even subtle physical traits of its namesake sharper eyes, quieter footsteps, faster reflexes, a pull toward the sky that never quite leaves them even standing on solid ground.

[[Skyreach]] does not farm much, and it does not trade often. It **hunts.** The mountains around it are home to beasts too large, too old, or too strange for ordinary folk to survive and so the people of [[Skyreach]] trained themselves, generation after generation, into something between soldiers and monster hunters. Every child is raised knowing that one day they will be expected to walk into the wilderness and come back having proven they can protect what's theirs.
### House Hawk

Kass was born into **House Hawk**, a family known less for brute strength than for patience, precision, and an almost unsettling stillness before they strike. Hawk-blooded Aasimar are the scouts and skirmishers of Skyreach sent ahead of hunting parties to track, to mark weaknesses, and to strike the first, often decisive, blow before vanishing back into the brush. It suited Kass from the moment he could walk. He was small for his age, quick, endlessly restless, and far more interested in climbing the aerie's outer crags than sitting through the village's lessons in stillness and prayer.

As is tradition for every child of Skyreach coming into their adolescence, Kass was paired with a companion of his House's bird, a hawk chick pulled from a wild nest and raised alongside him from a young age, so the two would imprint and grow together as something closer to siblings than pet and master. Kass named his companion **[[Skree]]**, for the sound it made incessantly as a hatchling. Where Kass is careful and watching, Skree is sharp and impatient the two balance each other in a way that even the elder hunters of House Hawk have taken notice of.

### The Long Flight

At the edge of adulthood, every child of Skyreach undergoes **the Long Flight**  a rite of passage where they are sent from the village, companion at their side, to hunt progressively larger and more dangerous game on their own. It isn't just a test of skill. It's the moment the village decides whether you are ready to be trusted with its protection. Those who return are no longer children. Those who don't are mourned, but never stopped Skyreach cannot afford to raise hunters who hesitate.

Kass's Long Flight began the way most do: proving himself against the smaller predators that stalk the lower slopes — wolves, giant vultures, the occasional wandering owlbear. But where most hunters are content to complete their Flight and return home to settle into the rhythm of village life, Kass hasn't stopped. He trained further. He took on soldier's duty at the village's outer watch, standing guard through the long mountain nights, learning discipline and formation-fighting alongside skills no ranger's manual ever taught him. how to hold a line, how to read a battlefield, how to keep still when everything in him wants to move.

Because Kass has heard the older hunters speak, in low voices, of a beast that no hunter of Skyreach has ever successfully brought down something older than the village itself, spoken of more as legend than fact, blamed for tragedies generations past that the village elders don't like to discuss in front of the young. Nobody has agreed to hunt it in Kass's lifetime. Nobody has proven themselves ready.

Kass intends to be the first.

### Kass Today

He is still young  not yet fully grown into the reputation he wants for himself and he knows it. That's part of why he left Skyreach's borders again after his soldier duty ended, venturing out into the wider world with Skree riding his shoulder or circling overhead: to hunt bigger, harder, stranger things than the mountain ever offered him, to sharpen every skill he has, and to come back one day having earned the right to face what no one else in his House ever has.

He carries himself like a hunter first and a soldier second quiet, watchful, quick to notice details others miss, more comfortable at the edge of a group scouting ahead than in the middle of a crowd. But the discipline from his soldier days shows too: he doesn't panic under pressure, and he's used to taking orders and holding a position when the moment calls for it, even if his instincts scream at him to move.
### Quick Reference

**Companion:** Skree, a hawk raised alongside Kass since hatchlinghood sharp-tempered, fiercely loyal, impatient with anything slower than flight.

**Personality Trait:** Kass goes quiet and still right before he acts the calm of a hunter who has already decided what happens next.

**Ideal:** _Proof._ Skill means nothing until it's tested against something worthy of it.

**Bond:** Somewhere out there is a beast his village has never been able to kill. Kass intends to be the one who finally does.

**Flaw:** His ambition outpaces his experience he's more willing to take on something dangerous than his skills currently justify, and pride keeps him from admitting when he's in over his head.


## 👨‍🎤 Description

Kass stands lean and sharp-angled, built more for speed than raw power — a frame shaped by a life spent on cliffs and wind currents rather than solid ground. What marks him immediately as Aarakocra, and as House Hawk specifically, is the crest of feathers sweeping back from his head — deep blue fading into white at the crown, more plumage than hair, faintly luminous the way all celestial-blooded features tend to be in low light. His face carries the sharpness of his namesake bird: hooded amber eyes, a narrow, hawkish set to his brow, an expression that reads as permanently watchful even at rest.

His armor is built for a hunter who needs to move without being heard — layered leather and bronze-worked plates over a body wrapped in overlapping feather-like scales that blend seamlessly from his shoulders down into an actual mantle of dark blue and slate-grey wings at his back. A teal scarf, wound high around his neck and lower face, doubles as both House colors and practical cover against high-altitude wind. Bronze clasp-work and geometric metal filigree — House Hawk's sigil worked into the largest piece at his collar — mark him as trained, not self-taught.

His weapons are twin curved blades, each wreathed in a faint blue glow reminiscent of his celestial blood — more talon than sword in silhouette, suited to the fast, precise strikes his House is known for rather than head-on combat. Clawed, taloned feet complete the look, more bird of prey than man, letting him grip cliff faces and uneven ground the way none of the lowlanders he occasionally guides ever quite get used to.

And almost always somewhere close by — perched on his shoulder, circling above, or waiting on a nearby outcrop with the same watchful stillness he has is [[Skree]], his hawk companion, smaller and plainer of feather than Kass himself but unmistakably cut from the same instincts.
