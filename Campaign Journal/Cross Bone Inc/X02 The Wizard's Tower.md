## 💡Key Learnings

- [[Lilith]] joined the party; she is 112 years old but has a condition preventing her from ageing
- [[Perseus Perchance]] is a wizard who spent centuries condensing Ley line energy inside his tower
- Ley lines are ancient magical sources crisscrossing the world; only certain individuals can tap them
- Siphoning even a sliver of Perseus's condensed ley line energy destabilised the entire power source and destroyed the tower
- [[Toodle]] is now powered by ley line energy rather than her original power source
- Perseus left with [[Matilda]] to find a new ley line source; the Wizard's Tower is completely destroyed


## 🤝 Who Did We Meet?

**[[Lilith]]** - New party member; appears as a small child but claims to be 112 years old and unable to age; a Ranger seeking to raise money
**Jacinta** - The Stuff Merchant; runs a lucky dip table of mystery bags, no returns
**Ham Door Stopper** - A retired adventurer and pub regular; the only local who had visited the tower, years ago; made it to the first floor before a second locked door stopped him
**[[Eman]]** - Mayor of Thornhaven; provided the party horses and rewarded them for investigating the light
**[[Perseus Perchance]]** - A centuries-old wizard and scientist; sole resident of the tower; specialises in Ley line research
**[[Matilda]]** - An Otyugh living in the tower basement; Perseus's beloved pet, dressed with pink bows on her tentacles


## 📝 Session Notes

- The party is in the [[Cross Bones Pub]], recovering from the adventures of [[S06 Chaos in the Garden]]
- A strange, unsettling figure entered and approached the party's table
	- Turned out to be [[Lilith]] — child-sized but 112 years old, with a condition that stopped her from ageing
	- She wanted to join the party to earn money; her interview answer: *"Raise money, and I don't care how"*
	- She was welcomed aboard, though the party immediately debated giving her a title
	- She is vampire but can walk in the daylight, as long as she is not under direct sunlight
- Nearby, **Jacinta** the Stuff Merchant offered lucky dip bags (10 gp each, d10 roll)
	- [[Nyra]]— **Quibbling Quill**: self-inking quill that auto-corrects your spelling, but sometimes rewrites whole sentences if it *disagrees* with what you wrote
	- [[Jinx]] — a **vial of blood** (unknown origin), **14 gp**, and a fresh **onion**
	- [[Lilith]] — the **Loyal Umbrella**: full-size umbrella that springs from the bag; once opened, it hovers above her and follows her for 24 hours; cannot be closed before then
	- [[Snag Ironvow]] — the **Sunny Side Stone**: a smooth pebble, always faintly warm, glows like a candle when held
	- [[Jinx]] — the **Sock of Endless Warmth**: a single sock; whatever is inside it stays warm and dry regardless of weather
- A massive crack of thunder filled the night; a bright blue lightning bolt struck the outskirts of town, followed by a sustained beam of blue light from the direction of the old Wizard's Tower
	- The whole town came outside to watch
	- The party tried and failed to locate the librarian (a young Dragonborn) in the crowd

**Gathering Information**
- At the pub the party found **Ham Door Stopper**, who had adventured out to the tower long ago
	- He described it as completely abandoned, "all dust," no signs of anyone
	- There was a central chamber with a large door he could not open; he decided it wasn't worth the risk and left
	- He had since retired, married, and had no interest in returning to adventuring or to the tower
	- The wizard's tower is far, they should ride horses instead of walking there
- [[Nyra]] went out to gather provisions
	- 2x healing potions for [[Snag Ironvow|Snag]]
	- 1x healing potion for [[Nyra]]
	- Rations for everyone

**Heading to the Tower**
- Mayor [[Eman]] loaned the party one horse and one pony; she had never been out to the tower herself
- With [[Nyra]]'s navigation skills, the party arrived in about two hours
- The tower was **brand new** — cleanly cut stone, decorative copper wires tracing up the exterior, a copper tile roof, no plant growth; nothing like the ruin Ham described
- A beam of blue light sat fixed above the tower like a spotlight

**Exploring the Tower**
- The front door was unlocked; inside was a cosy, well-stocked living room — clearly someone's home
- A second large door on the opposite wall was **locked solid**; a hammer swing, a strength check failed to open it. [[Jinx]] casts Thunderwave, hitting [[Nyra]] and [[Snag Ironvow|Snag]] in the process, the door does not budge.
- Upstairs: a tidy bedroom with robes, crystals, and books
	- [[Snag Ironvow]] found a copy of *Power Sources of the Far West* and a **handwritten journal** belonging to **[[Perseus Perchance]]**
		- Journal entries had a long gap, then resumed recently
		- Almost every entry mentioned "Matilda" with clear affection (e.g. "Took a walk with Matilda today")
		- Recent entries described being close to "unlocking the greatest power known to man"
	- [[Snag Ironvow]] also pocketed two **power source gems** — crystals clearly primed to receive magical energy
- Downstairs: the locked door has a sign that says "[[Matilda]]" on it
	- Inside is a large cavern carved beneath the tower, lit by blue torches, full of rubbish and old food waste
	- [[Matilda]] was here — an enormous Otyugh with pink bows tied around her tentacles
	- She grappled [[Snag Ironvow]] when he attempted to sneak past her
	- [[Jinx]] used **Speak with Animals** to communicate; Matilda could only convey basic wants ("Me hungry")
	- The party calmed her with rations and [[Jinx]]'s lucky dip onion
	- [[Jinx]] cast **Detect Magic** and found two magical signatures: a trapdoor in the cavern ceiling (30 ft up), and a **painting** on the ground floor

**The Painting Portal**
- The painting depicted a bright, window-filled room full of alchemical equipment — the kind of workspace the living room wasn't
- Stepping through the painting felt like a brief dip in a cold pool; on the other side was [[Perseus Perchance]]'s private laboratory
- Perseus was working at his bench, startled to find visitors
	- He had deliberately put his tower far from settlement and had no idea Thornhaven existed nearby
	- He explained ley line research: ancient magical sources run beneath the world; he had spent centuries condensing this energy, stored as a ball of concentrated blue lightning inside a copper cage
	- He was "so close" to stabilising it into a usable power source

**Toodle Repowered**
- [[Jinx]] showed Perseus [[Toodle]], who had been powered down since [[X01 Mr Mayfair's Magical Mystorium]]
- Perseus extracted a sliver of ley line energy and installed it in [[Toodle]]
- [[Toodle]] activated and immediately asked after Mr Mayfair and his daughter
- The sliver removed from the mass **destabilised the entire power source**

**Tower Collapse**
- The tower began reverting to its pre-renovation ruined state as the ley energy dissipated
- The painting has fallen over but [[Snag Ironvow]] was able to lift it up
- The party escaped back through the painting and out of the collapsing tower
- Perseus retrieved [[Matilda]] using a magical collar and got out safely
- He refused to come into Thornhaven; he intended to immediately search for a new ley line source
- The Wizard's Tower is now completely destroyed

**Return to Thornhaven**
- The party arrived back at dawn; the blue light was gone and the town was relieved
- [[Eman]] rewarded the party with **50 gp each**
