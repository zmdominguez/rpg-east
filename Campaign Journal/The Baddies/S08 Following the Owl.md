## 💡Key Learnings

- All four party members woke from the same blood root dream: heat, weight, darkness, the sensation of being pressed underground — each slightly different but unmistakably the same source
- [[Sera Voss]] approached [[Aeris Whisperwind]] alone at dawn — she was at the Elarion high court the night Thalen Rook gave his final performance. She pressed a torn page from one of Thalen's private manuscripts into Aeris's hand and gave a 3-day deadline: the Fat Pig Tavern, come alone, ask for Sera Voss
- At a stone bridge outside Thornhaven: six-legged tracks, week-old, pressed into the mud on one bank — a large creature crossed and did not continue on the other side
- [[Selune]]'s root-sense — already expanded by the blood root ritual — amplified the moment she stepped into the forest lake. The entire root network connected at once, like going from one instrument to a full orchestra
- The webs in [[Halfheight Forest]] are alarm silk, not trap silk. Their anchor points carry a faint crimson taint — blood root energy, expressed in the silk of creatures that have been consuming the root network for months
- [[Selune]] cast Speak with Animals on the Root Beetles mid-combat; the larger one responded: *"You are on our nest. You should suffer for entering. But you are going close to the Queen — we cannot allow you."* The Queen is somewhere deeper in the forest, where the party is headed
- The owl that guided the party to [[Halfheight Forest]] died during combat — killed by the Carapace Burst explosion when the Alpha fell
- A tree (named Celestia by [[Selune]]) pointed them toward the druid circle via Speak with Plants. Selune recognized the forest's druid song and knew the direction. The circle is where the eclipse ritual will happen


## 🤝 Who Did We Meet?

**[[Sera Voss]].** A woman in travel clothes who intercepted [[Aeris Whisperwind]] alone at dawn, before the party left Thornhaven. She says she was present at the Elarion high court the night Thalen Rook performed for the last time. She offered no explanation — only a torn manuscript page, a tavern name, and three days. She does not want to be seen. Whatever she has been carrying about that night, she has held it for a long time.


## 📝 Session Notes

- **Blood root dreams (pre-dawn):**
	- All four woke from the same class of dream — heat pressing in from all directions, darkness low and close, the feeling of being underground without being buried
	- [[Selune]]: unable to stand up, pressure from above. [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]]: hands shaking, mouth dry, every breath effort, no memory of content — only that she couldn't get out. [[Mirr]]: woke on the floor with no memory of moving there. [[Kass]]: woke with unexplained dirt on his hands
	- None of them mentioned it immediately
	- Self-check on waking: mentally clear, not drunk or exhausted — but not their usual selves. Something is changing, slowly

- **Sera Voss at dawn:**
	- [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]] was briefly alone loading gear — forty seconds, maybe less
	- [[Sera Voss]] approached from across the street, hood up, fast but unhurried
	- *"I was there. At the high court in Elarion. The last night your father performed."*
	- She pressed a torn page into Aeris's hand: a page from one of Thalen Rook's private manuscripts — not a court commission, personal work. Aeris recognized the handwriting from childhood. The page cuts off mid-sentence; one corner carries a brown stain, old ink or dried blood
	- *"Three days. The Fat Pig Tavern. Come alone. Ask for Sera Voss."*
	- She was already walking before she finished the name. Did not look back
	- Aeris recognised the paper — the kind her father used. Someone who knew his private work well enough to carry a page of it was watching her perform at the [[Cross Bones Pub]] last night and chose dawn, alone, to make contact
	- Aeris kept this to herself for now

- **Aeris's short rest performance:**
	- Before departing, [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]] performed a brief song — Inspiring Leader (level + CHA = 9 temporary HP) for the party, then distributed Bardic Inspiration dice

- **The owl leads out:**
	- Arrived at first light, crossed the road flying low — close enough to touch — then banked and led west out of the city
	- It did not circle or wait. It moved like something doing a job

- **Travel to [[Halfheight Forest]]:**
	- About 4 miles on foot, two hours; the final stretch walked — horses left at the treeline, too uneasy to go further
	- At a stone bridge over a narrow stream: six-legged tracks in the bank mud, heavy and wide-spaced, roughly a week old. Stride was purposeful. The trail goes into the stream and does not continue on the other side
	- No natural territory for something this large this close to Thornhaven
	- [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]] saved Bardic Inspiration for inside the forest (only lasts an hour)

- **Entering [[Halfheight Forest]]:**
	- The outer trees are ordinary. Twenty feet in they change — trunks go near-black, the canopy locks together, ferns and root arches replace undergrowth. Roots rise above the earth in shapes too deliberate to be natural
	- Air smells of wet soil, old wood, and something faintly sweet that isn't blood root but is related to it
	- [[Selune]] felt the root network connect the moment she crossed the treeline. Not individual signals — a whole crowd, all speaking at once. She could not make out voices. Only the weight of them
	- Aeris distributed Bardic Inspiration to the party before going deeper

- **The lake:**
	- Small, dark, still. A dead branch arches over the water. The owl perched on it without moving
	- [[Selune]] stepped in — Perception 17 — and the root-sense expanded into the whole forest at once. Like going from hearing a single instrument to standing inside a full orchestra mid-performance. Everything connected, everything present
	- Shadows fell at the wrong angles relative to the light. No birdsong in this section of the forest — not a pause, a sustained absence
	- Across the water: the owl, balanced perfectly in the centre of a web sheet between two root arches, not touching a single thread

- **The alarm webs:**
	- Large pale sheets strung between root arches at ground level and into the lower branches — alarm silk, not trap silk (different from the Thelasian webs encountered in [[S03 The Thelasian Webs]])
	- At the anchor points where silk met bark, a faint crimson sheen — blood root energy, expressed in the silk after months of the beetles feeding on the root junctions below
	- The owl's position showed a line through. The party agreed: everyone go through, no alarm triggered
	- [[Mirr]] used Acrobatics to navigate the gap — rolled 17, cleared it cleanly
	- [[Selune]] Wild Shaped small to fit through, then returned to natural form on the other side
	- One Strength saving throw mid-crossing — rolled 13, barely made it (left ankle moment, no mechanical effect)
	- All through without triggering the alarm

- **Wombat-sized creature:**
	- Waiting just inside the web boundary, moving slowly. Passive Perception 22
	- Attempted communication — indifferent, not hostile
	- It moved on. No conflict

- **Root Beetle combat:**
	- One large worker first erupted and attacked [[Selune]] (bat-sized); two more surfaced as combat continued; the Alpha surfaced later via Subterranean Strike
	- Workers: 1d8+3 piercing + poison on hit. [[Selune]] took 10 poison damage and became Poisoned early
	- [[Kass]] opened combat with psychic daggers — 14 (miss), 8 (hit, marked the target)
	- [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]] cast Blindness/Deafness (DC 15 Con save) — beetle rolled 14, failed. Blinded. Aeris then repositioned away from the cluster; the blinded worker fell prone trying to disengage, granting Aeris an opportunity attack (advantage, rolled 21, 17 damage) — it crawled underground
	- Alpha surfaced and immediately triggered Carapace Burst — spore cloud, DC 12 Con save. Two party members failed and became Poisoned; one passed for half damage (6)
	- [[Mirr]] summoned her Pact of the Blade Warhammer, hit roll 25, 11 damage, PUSH — shoved the Alpha toward [[Kass]]
	- A new worker appeared and bit a Poisoned character — 10 piercing + Con save with disadvantage: failed, 19 poison damage
	- [[Aeris Whisperwind|Aeris]] cast Healing Word (bonus action) to bring a downed character back up; later cast 3rd-level Mass Healing Word (bonus action) — healed all five (4 party + owl) for 10 HP each
	- [[Mirr]] used her Goliath Large Form species trait (advantage on Strength, +10 speed) to grapple a worker beetle (Strength check, roll 16), then flipped it onto its back — prone
	- [[Mirr]] switched her Pact of the Blade from Warhammer to Greatsword; rolled a Nat 20, dealing 4d6 (doubled dice) = 15 damage, then a bonus attack from the crit (20 hit, 6 more damage)
	- [[Selune]] attempted Speak with Animals mid-combat — spoke to the larger beetle far from the group: *"Please stop attacking us. I'm a druid. I'm trying to get to the circle of this forest."*
	- The beetle responded: *"We don't care about you. You are on our nest. You should suffer for entering."* Selune countered that they were just passing. Beetle: *"But you are going close to the Queen. We cannot allow you."*
	- [[Selune]] then Wild Shaped into a black bear and killed the prone beetle with multi-attack (21 hit, rolled 17+21 hit); the beetle's carapace burst on death — spore cloud again. All four made Con saves with disadvantage (three of four Poisoned). The owl, still perched nearby, failed its save and **died from the explosion**
	- [[Kass]] used Hunter's Mark on the Alpha (bonus action), attacked with psychic daggers — total 6 damage
	- [[Mirr]] cast Hex on the last surviving worker (disadvantage on Strength, +3 necrotic on hit), attacked with dagger — 25 hit, 7 piercing + 3 necrotic. Beetle fled underground; Mirr's opportunity attack missed
	- Combat ended. Multiple characters at very low HP; one character reached 7 HP at lowest point

- **The beetle dialogue (via Speak with Animals):**
	- The beetles are guarding their nest — the party walked into it without knowing
	- The "Queen" is somewhere deeper in the forest, in the direction the party is heading toward the druid circle
	- The beetles did not pursue after the last one fled underground

- **After combat — loot and the dead owl:**
	- The owl that guided them into the forest died in the final Carapace Burst. The party recovered the body
	- [[Mirr]] cut a horn from the Alpha's carapace — 20cm piece (half of the original 45cm), could be crafted into a dagger by the right smith; will rot without preservation
	- Nature check (roll 11): 8 plates of carapace, ~30cm each — enough for armor crafting or sale
	- Party decided to bring the dead owl's body with them; [[Mirr]] fashioned a small nest to carry it in
	- Party yelled into the forest in the general direction the owl had been flying — no answer came back

- **Following toward the circle:**
	- The party walked away from the nest area until [[Selune]]'s root-sense returned (the "music" of the forest coming back underfoot)
	- [[Selune]] used Speak with Plants (Moonpetal Circlet, 1/LR) on a nearby tree — Selune named it Celestia. Celestia told them: *"During the night you can follow the moon's direction and you're going to find the druids."*
	- The moon came out above the canopy
	- Selune recognized the forest's druid-circle song in the roots: *"I know how it sounds because I've been to a circle before. The song of the druids. They would know the eclipse is coming. The circle would be where that ritual happens."*
	- Session ended with the party making camp to rest before following the moon toward the druid circle, dead owl in tow
